About DEFENCE PATH

"Your Dream. Your Discipline. Your Uniform."

Our Core Mission

DEFENCE PATH is a centralized, digital-first defence career intelligence, verified notification directory, and authentic examination preparation portal designed specifically for Indian youth seeking entry into the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, Indian Coast Guard, and Central Armed Police Forces (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, and Assam Rifles).

Every year, millions of passionate aspirants dream of donning the uniform. However, fragmented notification portals, outdated question papers, unclear eligibility rules, and predatory test engines hinder their preparation. Defence Path bridges this void with authentic data, official Gazetted cutoff guidelines, and a real-time computer-based testing simulation engine.

Authentic CBT Mocks

Countdown timers, official marking schemes (+4 / -1.33, +1 / -0.33), question palettes, and bilingual views mirroring UPSC and CDAC test environments.

Weakness Analytics

Surgical identification of weak subjects, chapters, and topics based on actual test records without fabricated metrics.

13 Indian Languages

National accessibility spanning English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, and Urdu.
