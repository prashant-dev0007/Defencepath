ASPIRANT CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Defence Path an official government recruitment website?

No. Defence Path is an independent educational portal engineered to help aspirants discover authentic recruitment updates, understand complex eligibility criteria, and practice authentic PYQs and mock tests. All applications must be submitted directly through official government portals (such as joinindianarmy.nic.in, upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in).

Are the mock test marking schemes aligned with actual exams?

Yes. Defence Path CBT engine calculates scores strictly in accordance with official examination patterns — including negative marking (e.g., -0.83 for NDA Mathematics, -0.33 for CDS, -0.5 for Agniveer GD, and -1.0 for AFCAT).

How does the weakness analysis work?

The weakness analysis system evaluates your recorded test attempts from the database. It categorizes subjects, chapters, and topics based on your accuracy: Strong (> 70%), Practice Recommended (50% – 70%), and Needs Improvement (< 50%). It prevents false conclusions by checking for minimum attempt thresholds.

Can I access Defence Path in regional Indian languages?

Yes. Defence Path supports 13 Indian languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, and Urdu (with complete RTL orientation). Select your preferred language in the top navbar.
